    Schedule & limits

    Each morning's round

    Candidates of each kind; keep the best one of each for today's posts.

    Clips sorted as "other" (beach, random moments) are never picked automatically. You can still make a video from any clip in Your clips.

    Text on video

    POV: the energy shift when we pull into the airport

    Best practice: keep the text to 2–3 short lines, readable at a glance, and clear of TikTok's buttons (right side) and caption (bottom). Medium fits most videos; use Large for very short lines and Small for long ones.

    Themes for videos

    Every clip is sorted into a theme. Tap a theme to switch it on or off: only footage from themes that are on goes into videos. The numbers are clips not used yet / all clips.

    Travel days

    The daily fun and educational videos.

    Beyond the airport

    One video a day (the inspirational one) is made from these: places, walks, nature, moments together.

    Places

    Where the footage was filmed, from the position your phone saves in each video (or signs and landmarks). Switch a country off to keep it out of videos.

    Situations & TikTok captions

    Clips are sorted into these situations. For each one, captions that did well on TikTok are researched automatically and adapted to your clips. You can add captions you found yourself.

    Story video

    Write what you'll say. To pick the footage, type / and the scene (or pick one you used before): it becomes a tag for the words after it and is never spoken. Words without a scene get the best-matching clip automatically.

    Voiceover

    Labs

    Beta features. For now Rollcut focuses on getting the daily educational, fun and inspirational videos right; these are extras you can switch on to try. Each shows up in Settings when it's on.

    Day vlog

    The whole day in quick moments: days with at least 15 clips. Look through the clips, switch off any you don't want, and the AI cuts 15–20 of them into a fast 35–45s vlog in the order you filmed them, with a headline and a few lines. Vlogs are only made when you ask, never in the daily videos. They go to Review like the others.

    What people search

    What people search for, grouped into topics per theme. Every other educational video answers the most-searched topic of its theme that isn't covered yet, in the searchers' own words, so it comes up for those searches. Searches that aren't about traveling with a dog are left out.

    Two sources, each kept until you add a newer one: TikTok (TikTok Studio → Analytics → Search → Export: the zip) for what brought people to your videos, and Google (Google Ads Keyword Planner → download keyword ideas: the .csv) for what everyone searches.

    Trending sounds

    The 10 sounds being picked up fastest on TikTok in travel, airport and dog posts: new posts using each sound per day in the last 3 days against the 11 before (a hockey stick, not just big). Read every 3 days (about 240 posts, under $1.20 each time; usually far less). Each video's details suggest the ones that fit it.

      From your TikTok

      Reads your posts of the last days you pick: what people want to know in your comments becomes topics, however they put it (comments under 4 words, emojis, tags and your own replies are skipped; compliments and jokes don't count), and the posts people saved and shared most guide the lessons. Up to 3 replies per comment are read too (follow-up questions hide there). About $2–6 for 90 days, then a few cents a week to keep it fresh.

      Questions people ask

      Each educational video answers one of these, taking turns and matching the footage (the bag question with under-the-seat clips, and so on). Write your real answers: they're the only source for brand names, airlines, places and rules. Unanswered questions get a general answer that names nothing.

      Brand partners

      The brands you work with. On any video, tap Collab version to make a variation built around one of them: text, cut and description, with the mention and #ad.

        Videos that worked

        Past videos that performed well. Their tone and text style guide new captions.

        Each link is read (caption and plays) and connected to the video it came from, so its numbers teach the AI what works.

        What the server sends to your phone, mostly videos (the app plays light copies). Render includes 5 GB a month; beyond that it charges about $0.15 per GB (so 10 GB a month costs about $0.75 extra). At 90% of your limit, videos show as still images until the 1st; at 98%, saving and sharing stop too.

        AI spending covers both Claude (AI) and Apify (TikTok search). Claude's cost comes from the usage it reports after every call; Apify's is estimated on the high side per search result. The meter updates after every call. When the limit is reached, the app stops calling both until next month. For a hard cap, also set limits in the Anthropic Console and in Apify.
