Put the videos you want to use in shared Google Photos albums and paste their links here, as many as you like. New videos are picked up every few hours, and a video that's in more than one album is only imported once.

    Tip: make the album add Lex by itself: in Google Photos open the album, ⋮ → Options → Automatically add → People & pets → Lex. New albums use videos from the last 30 days. Rollcut keeps about five rounds' worth of clips sorted and ready, importing more as you review.

    Schedule & limits

    Each morning's round

    Candidates of each kind; keep the best one of each for today's posts.

    Clips sorted as "other" (beach, random moments) are never picked automatically. You can still make a video from any clip in Your clips.

    Text on video

    POV: the energy shift when we pull into the airport

    Best practice: keep the text to 2–3 short lines, readable at a glance, and clear of TikTok's buttons (right side) and caption (bottom). Medium fits most videos; use Large for very short lines and Small for long ones.

    Situations & TikTok captions

    Clips are sorted into these situations. For each one, captions that did well on TikTok are researched automatically and adapted to your clips. You can add captions you found yourself.

    Story video

    Write what you'll say. To pick the footage, type / and the scene (or pick one you used before): it becomes a tag for the words after it and is never spoken. Words without a scene get the best-matching clip automatically.

    Voiceover

    Questions people ask

    Each educational video answers one of these, taking turns and matching the footage (the bag question with under-the-seat clips, and so on). Write your real answers: they're the only source for brand names, airlines, places and rules. Unanswered questions get a general answer that names nothing.

    Brand partners

    The brands you work with. On any video, tap Collab version to make a variation built around one of them: text, cut and description, with the mention and #ad.
